How to Use This Map

Every pain point and opportunity opens a definition and the evidence behind it — what the item actually means, and how it was measured, whether that was analytics, session replays, usability testing or support-ticket tagging. Each card states its own basis, so you can tell a well-evidenced problem from an assumption before deciding what to fund.

🔧 Work is underway — the card carries its Jira ticket, owner, timeline and expected impact
🔵 In Progress — actively being built, team assigned
🟡 Planned — scheduled to start soon
✅ Launching Soon — ready or launching within a week
⬆️ ➡️ ⬇️ Priority — high, medium or low, shown on every card
Reading the GA Trends

Each figure carries its movement against the previous four-week average. The arrow shows which way the number went; the colour shows whether that is good or bad news — so a falling bounce rate is green, while rising support contacts are red. Hover any indicator for the exact change.
